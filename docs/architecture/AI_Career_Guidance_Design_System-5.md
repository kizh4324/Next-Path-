# Design System – AI-Based Personalized Career Guidance

**Purpose**  
This design system defines the visual language, UI components, accessibility principles, and screen structure for an AI-based personalized career guidance system for students.

## 1. Design Goals

- Simple and student-friendly
- Modern and AI-focused
- Trustworthy and easy to understand
- Mobile-first and accessible
- Personalized and progress-oriented

## 2. Core User Flow

- Student Profile → AI Analysis → Career Recommendations → Skill Gap Detection → Personalized Roadmap → Courses & Certifications → Projects → Internship → Job

## 3. Colour Palette

- Primary: Blue – trust, education and technology
- Secondary: Purple – AI and innovation
- Success: Green – completed skills and achievements
- Warning: Orange – skill gaps and areas needing attention
- Background: White / Light Grey
- Text: Dark Navy / Black

## 4. Typography

- Primary font: Poppins or Inter
- Headings: Bold
- Subheadings: Semi-Bold
- Body text: Regular
- Use short, readable text and clear hierarchy.

## 5. UI Components

- Navigation Bar – Home, Career, Roadmap, Courses and Profile
- Career Cards – career title, match score, explanation and next action
- Skill Cards – current skills and missing skills
- Progress Bars – learning and roadmap progress
- Recommendation Cards – courses, certifications and projects
- Career Match Score – visual percentage indicating suitability
- Roadmap Timeline – Current Level → Skills → Courses → Projects → Internship → Job
- AI Chatbot – conversational career guidance
- Charts – academic performance, skills and career analysis
- Language Selector – supports English and regional languages

## 6. Key Screens

- Onboarding: collect education, interests, skills, academic performance, preferences, goals, budget and available study time.
- Dashboard: show career matches, overall progress, skill gaps and recommended actions.
- Career Recommendation: show career match percentage, reasons, required skills and job-market relevance.
- Skill Gap: compare current skills with target-career requirements.
- Career Roadmap: present learning steps from current level to job readiness.
- Courses & Certifications: recommend learning resources based on knowledge, budget, time and career goal.
- AI Career Counselor: allow students to ask questions about careers, skills and learning paths.
- Profile & Settings: manage student information, preferences and language.

## 7. Accessibility

- Use readable font sizes and strong text contrast.
- Use icons together with labels instead of icons alone.
- Keep navigation simple and consistent.
- Provide multilingual support.
- Design for mobile screens first.
- Avoid complex technical terminology where possible.

## 8. Design Principles

- Personalization – recommendations should reflect the student's profile.
- Clarity – explain why a career is recommended.
- Progress – make learning progress visible.
- Actionability – every recommendation should have a clear next step.
- Accessibility – make guidance usable for students from different backgrounds.
- Trust – avoid forcing a single career choice; provide explanations and alternatives.

## 9. Example Dashboard Content

- Hi, Student 👋
- Career Match: Data Scientist – 87%
- Other Matches: UI/UX Designer – 78%, Software Engineer – 74%
- Progress: 80%
- Skills to Improve: Python, SQL, Statistics
- Primary CTA: View My Recommendations
- Secondary CTA: Start Skill Roadmap
- Floating Action: Ask Career AI

## 10. Design System Tagline

- “Your Career, Your Path.”
- The experience should feel like a personal career companion rather than a traditional education portal.

## 11. Source Basis

This document is based on the uploaded hackathon problem statement, which describes personalized career recommendation, skill-gap detection, career roadmaps, course and certification recommendations, job-market trends, AI counseling, accessibility, financial constraints, project recommendations, resume/interview support and multilingual guidance.
